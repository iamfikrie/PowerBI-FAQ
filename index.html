Frequently Asked Questions

Please find below answers to common questions regarding the use and functionality of this portal.

Q1: How often is the data in the dashboard updated?

Data currently refreshes every 30 minutes. We are actively working to optimize our data pipelines to reduce this interval and will provide updates as we improve performance.

Q2: I submitted a job via the form. When will it appear in the portal?

Your submission will appear in the portal within 30 minutes, depending on the time of submission. Our system runs on a half-hour refresh cycle (e.g., 11:00, 11:30, 12:00, 12:30, etc.). Please rest assured that your submission is securely tracked; in the rare event a submission fails to reach the operations team, our internal team is automatically notified to resolve the issue.

Q3: Does the 30-minute dashboard refresh delay the start of my job?

No. While the dashboard refreshes every 30 minutes, our operations team retrieves new submissions every 15 minutes. This means our team begins reviewing your request well before it appears as "updated" on your portal. We have structured our workflow this way to ensure your projects are prioritized and processed as efficiently as possible.

Q4: What should I do if I have an urgent request that requires immediate attention?

For urgent matters, please contact our team in Malaysia directly. This ensures we are immediately aware of your request and can prioritize it outside of the standard dashboard refresh cycle.

Q5: Will the refresh interval be faster in the future?

Yes. Our team is dedicated to enhancing the portal's efficiency. We are continuously working on backend improvements and will notify you as soon as we are able to support more frequent, near-real-time updates.

Q6: Can I edit or cancel a job after it has been submitted?

Currently, changes to submitted jobs should be handled by contacting our team in Malaysia directly. This ensures that any adjustments are correctly synchronized with our operational workflow and that no updates are missed.
